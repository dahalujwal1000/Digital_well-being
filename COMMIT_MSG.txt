v1.1.2: F5 + Facebook-style pull-to-refresh (wheel overscroll)

- F5 anywhere refreshes the full dashboard
- Wheel-up overscroll at the top of any tab emulates pull-to-refresh:
  1st tick shows "Pull again to refresh", 2nd tick within 0.7s shows a
  "Refreshing…" pill and re-renders, then "Updated ✓" (2s cooldown)
- Fixed a latent bug: both scrollable tabs registered bind_all("<MouseWheel>")
  without add="+", so the second registration (Sessions) silently replaced
  the first (Apps) and Apps-tab wheel scrolling never worked
